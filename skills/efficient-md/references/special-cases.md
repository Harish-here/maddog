# Special cases

## A text of no listed kind

A text of no listed kind takes the rules for every kind. Its keep test:
would the reader act differently without this line? Its move-out list is
the three ways to save. It has no alarm; the keep test alone decides its
length.

## Content that moves to another kind

When content moves to a text of another kind, write the new text by that
kind's recipe. The original stays under its own.
