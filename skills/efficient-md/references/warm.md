# WARM — per-dispatch briefs, specs, skill bodies

Laws: blank-context complete, minimally. Premium slots — attention is
U-shaped, so DONE-WHEN, commands, and laws live at top and bottom,
never mid-file. One canonical exemplar over three paragraphs of
description. Mark skippable sections "Optional". Ceiling ~500 lines;
deeper detail forks to references one level deep. IDs and paths over
prose; no narrative history. Living state files (resume records,
working plans) are WARM however rarely read — rewriting is their
function; COLD is for records of the past.

Exemplar (a brief's load-bearing frame — top and bottom carry it):

    PURPOSE: quote every retry clause with file:line so drift can be ruled on.
    DONE-WHEN: every service under services/ has its clause quoted or "none found".
    INPUTS: services/ (read-only). Optional background: services/README.md.
    ...body...
    NOTES (required): claims to verify, anomalies, anything blocked.
